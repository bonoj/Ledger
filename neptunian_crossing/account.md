# Neptunian Crossing

An account of a change in scale.

This crossing begins at the moment ammonia stopped being merely a verified molecular construction and became, in human perception, planetary:

> “Neptunian. Striking. Ammonia approved. Drop a new thoughtform from the betwixtable. Let's embed a cold boot to return instantly to this point. Probably helpful to keep a list of molecules so far and our working process. And throw a few tiny molecules in there, have them swirl and dance as the gimbal cage spins :). We are going to zoom out to Jupiter and do a quick cloud band simulation with coriolis effect and possibly material gas before we continue.”

That utterance is the boundary of this account. Earlier Chemlab work remains in `chemlab_crossing`; this account follows what happens after attention changes scale.

## Crossing state

Ammonia is accepted.

Chemlab has been put to sleep rather than concluded. A second Thoughtform hangs beneath the Betwixtable opposite the earlier semantic-spatial Thoughtform. It carries a cold boot containing the accepted molecular corpus:

- CH4
- H2O
- CO2
- C2H6
- C2H4
- C2H2
- NH3

It also carries the earned working loop:

`science fixture -> projection -> executable geometry regression -> browser/CI agreement -> Home pin -> Interstice runtime gate -> human inspection -> acceptance`

Tiny water, methane, and ammonia constructions orbit and tumble inside its glass gimbal cage. They are visual memory, not chemistry authority.

The cold boot preserves known debt without demanding attention: bond-order rendering lags graph semantics; the relational projector is still directed/tree-shaped rather than a cycle solver; semantic ports remain metadata rather than a general compatibility system; repeated specimen replacement still deserves explicit GPU-resource disposal.

Exact Betwixt provenance at departure from Chemlab: `52d7b0d7fa8753bfe9f2cf8ddb47aa52a10bf528`.

## Direction, not destination

The immediate move is outward to Jupiter.

The provisional experiment is intentionally small: a rotating spherical shell, latitude-dependent zonal flow, and tracer material, asking whether Coriolis structure and differential bands can produce something recognizably Jovian before “gas” earns any deeper material machinery.

This is not yet an Astrolab expedition or a predetermined planetary framework. The crossing is allowed to discover what it is.

### The Roci comes before Jupiter

The first published cold-boot Thoughtform exposed a browser parse failure: `SyntaxError: Unexpected token 'const'`. The static/executable chemistry gates had remained green because they verify the chemistry module and source agreement, not parse/execute the complete browser shell. The failure is localized to the newly inserted second Thoughtform: its declarations were accidentally placed after the `build(root)` method had already closed, leaving `const` declarations directly inside an object literal. The last runtime-proven Betwixt pin was restored while the source repair is made; the cold-boot payload remains preserved in Ledger and source history.

At the same turn, john sharpened the planetary direction:

So the Neptunian sequence is now: repair the shipyard, make a Roci, earn material gas, then use it against Jupiter and its cloud bands. The Jupiter experiment remains a destination, not the immediate construction.
