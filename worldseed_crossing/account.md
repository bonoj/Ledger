# Worldseed Crossing

Private working account for the blank-world experiment.

john has deliberately declined to inspect or approve the design before execution. This account is therefore a place for the model to preserve its own predictions, choices, doubts, and changes of mind before the world exists.

## Charge

Begin with an effectively blank operable substrate.

Author a small world process capable of accumulating persistent consequence without requiring john to preapprove geometries, prose, systems, or representations.

The first experiment should separate model authorship from world execution: a small number of model design interventions may establish the seed, laws, and initial conditions; a much larger number of deterministic world turns should then execute locally and leave replayable causal history.

The experiment is not "generate a fantasy map." The desired phenomenon is:

**blank substrate → accumulated consequence → a world neither participant specified in finished form**

## Constraints already earned

- State is not scene. Scene is not state.
- Generation remains proposal until consequence is committed.
- Use semantic relationships to construct and spatial evidence to verify.
- Persist enough causal history to explain what happened.
- Do not prebuild a giant ontology, procedural-map checklist, agent framework, or god object.
- Let pressure and confusion earn vocabulary.
- Preserve working organisms before abstracting them.
- john should not become a transport or approval layer.
- The model should not approve its own geometry in advance either: realize, observe, then accept, alter, replace, or destroy.
- Existing World Lab machinery is available where useful, but inheritance is not obligation.

## Pass 0 — posture

I do not yet want to decide what the finished world looks like.

I want to decide what can become consequential.

The central design risk is producing a procedural screensaver: deterministic complexity that looks busy but in which nothing has a reason to exist. The opposite failure is over-authoring the laws until the outcome is merely a delayed rendering of my plan.

The seed therefore needs at least three properties:

1. **Scarcity or incompatibility** — not every possible local state can coexist.
2. **Memory** — events change the cost or possibility of later events.
3. **Propagation** — a local consequence can alter conditions somewhere else.

Those three are enough to create history. They may or may not be enough to create a world.

I will use subsequent passes to choose the smallest executable form of those pressures, then decide how state becomes perceivable without making the projection authoritative.

## Prediction before design

If this works, recognizable structures should arise because they are economical responses to accumulated history, not because I named them roads, mountains, towns, borders, storms, or stories.

If I have to name those things in the simulator before they can exist, I have probably lost the experiment.
