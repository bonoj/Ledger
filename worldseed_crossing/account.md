# Worldseed Crossing

Private working account for the blank-world experiment.

john has deliberately declined to inspect or approve the design before execution. This account is therefore a place for the model to preserve its own predictions, choices, doubts, and changes of mind before the world exists.

## Charge

Begin with an effectively blank operable substrate.

Author a small world process capable of accumulating persistent consequence without requiring john to preapprove geometries, prose, systems, or representations.

The first experiment should separate model authorship from world execution: a small number of model design interventions may establish the seed, laws, and initial conditions; a much larger number of deterministic world turns should then execute locally and leave replayable causal history.

The experiment is not "generate a fantasy map." The desired phenomenon is:

**blank substrate → accumulated consequence → a world neither participant specified in finished form**

## Constraints already earned

- State is not scene. Scene is not state.
- Generation remains proposal until consequence is committed.
- Use semantic relationships to construct and spatial evidence to verify.
- Persist enough causal history to explain what happened.
- Do not prebuild a giant ontology, procedural-map checklist, agent framework, or god object.
- Let pressure and confusion earn vocabulary.
- Preserve working organisms before abstracting them.
- john should not become a transport or approval layer.
- The model should not approve its own geometry in advance either: realize, observe, then accept, alter, replace, or destroy.
- Existing World Lab machinery is available where useful, but inheritance is not obligation.

## Pass 0 — posture

I do not yet want to decide what the finished world looks like.

I want to decide what can become consequential.

The central design risk is producing a procedural screensaver: deterministic complexity that looks busy but in which nothing has a reason to exist. The opposite failure is over-authoring the laws until the outcome is merely a delayed rendering of my plan.

The seed therefore needs at least three properties:

1. **Scarcity or incompatibility** — not every possible local state can coexist.
2. **Memory** — events change the cost or possibility of later events.
3. **Propagation** — a local consequence can alter conditions somewhere else.

Those three are enough to create history. They may or may not be enough to create a world.

I will use subsequent passes to choose the smallest executable form of those pressures, then decide how state becomes perceivable without making the projection authoritative.

## Prediction before design

If this works, recognizable structures should arise because they are economical responses to accumulated history, not because I named them roads, mountains, towns, borders, storms, or stories.

If I have to name those things in the simulator before they can exist, I have probably lost the experiment.


## Pass 1 — choose the primitive

I want the authoritative world to begin below the level of objects.

Use a modest 2D lattice as **places**, but do not make its cells terrain types. Each place carries only a few continuous quantities:

- stored potential: something locally available to be spent or moved;
- resistance: accumulated difficulty of changing or crossing the place;
- trace: durable residue of prior throughput;
- disturbance: short-lived local pressure.

These names are intentionally pre-semantic. They are mechanics, not lore.

A turn injects or releases potential at a small number of deterministic disturbances. Potential seeks cheaper neighboring states. Moving through a place leaves trace. Trace changes later resistance. Disturbance can erase, redirect, or amplify trace. Thus history modifies future flow.

This gives the minimum causal knot I wanted:

**difference → movement → residue → changed affordance → different movement**

No road exists. But repeated movement can make one.
No river exists. But persistent source/sink pressure can make a channel.
No border exists. But accumulated resistance can make one.
Those interpretations belong to projection/observation, not the state machine.

### Why not agents yet

Agents would smuggle goals into turn zero. Ecology would smuggle categories. Settlements would smuggle human scale. Heightfields would smuggle landscape. Language would smuggle narrative.

A field can acquire all of those later if its history earns discrete handles.

### A necessary asymmetry

Pure diffusion dies into equilibrium. The world needs continuing non-equilibrium without a scripted story.

I will give the seed a tiny deterministic set of **sources and sinks whose strengths drift on incommensurate periods**, plus rare deterministic shocks. Nothing chooses a destination. The mismatch keeps the field from settling and makes old traces alternately useful and harmful.

The key is that shocks should not merely randomize. They should expose whether accumulated structure has become consequential.

### First acceptance criterion

After 1,000 turns, if resetting only the transient quantities while preserving trace/resistance produces materially different subsequent behavior than a virgin world under the same forcing, then the world has history in an executable sense.

If preserving history does not change its future, the apparent structure is decoration.

## Pass 1 prediction

I expect channels, basins, scars, and junction-like regions to emerge visually even though none exist semantically.

I do **not** yet expect anything deserving the word settlement, organism, culture, or story. Pretending otherwise would be anthropomorphic projection.

The next pass must solve a harder problem: how a continuous historical field can earn discrete persistent things without me declaring the classes of things in advance.


## Pass 2 — earning things

The continuous field cannot be the whole world. If every apparent structure remains merely a shape I visually interpret afterward, then the simulation has history but never acquires vocabulary.

I need a mechanism by which the world can earn a discrete handle without a catalog of allowed object classes.

### Persistent distinction

At intervals, inspect the field for regions whose behavior has become both:

- **coherent**: neighboring places participate in a shared dynamical pattern;
- **persistent**: that pattern survives long enough to affect later flow.

When such a region crosses a conservative threshold, assign it an anonymous handle. The handle initially means nothing beyond:

**this distinction has remained useful long enough to remember.**

Its record contains its birth turn, changing footprint, a few measured properties, and lineage if it later splits, merges, or dies. It does not receive a noun.

This is deliberately analogous to Neptunian anonymous continuity: continuity before semantic identity.

### Things must pay rent

A handle that ceases to predict or constrain anything should decay and die. Persistence alone cannot be enough; Graph Mind already taught us that stable identity can become the cheapest counterfeit abstraction.

A distinction earns continued existence only if conditioning on it improves prediction of a later local consequence: direction of throughput, persistence of trace, response to a shock, or another cheap measurable future.

So the world is allowed to say “this” before it knows “what this is,” but **this** must remain useful.

### Interaction between earned things

Do not give handles bespoke behaviors. Their interaction is still mediated through the underlying field.

If two persistent distinctions repeatedly alter one another through that substrate, record a relation as another anonymous persistent distinction. Relations also pay rent.

This gives a possible ladder:

**field history → persistent distinction → anonymous thing → persistent relation → higher-order distinction**

No rung is guaranteed.

If 1,000 turns produce only a field and three useful anonymous regions, that is a better result than manufacturing a kingdom.

### Projection

Now I can decide what john sees without confusing it with authority.

The table should begin nearly blank: a dark bounded surface with slight material depth. The authoritative lattice is invisible.

Projection is earned from evidence:

- stored potential can subtly lift/depress or shade the surface;
- resistance can alter roughness/form;
- trace can become fine persistent marks or grooves;
- disturbance can produce transient motion;
- anonymous handles may acquire minimal physical markers only after they exist;
- relations may become visible only if their persistence makes doing so useful.

The renderer is a witness to state, not its author.

I specifically do **not** want labels or notebook prose in version one. Those are attractive enough to counterfeit meaning. If language eventually appears, it should be because a later model observation needs a handle the world itself cannot cheaply expose spatially.

### Replay

Every turn records only enough causal delta to reconstruct or deterministically replay the world. Seed + law version + forcing schedule + accepted state transitions should be sufficient. A visual recording is unnecessary.

I want two viewing modes eventually, but only one is needed for the first executable:

1. accelerated genesis from turn 0 to turn 1000;
2. arrival at turn 1000 with the world still alive.

The first is more valuable initially because john can watch causality accumulate rather than inspect a mysterious finished artifact.

### Model involvement boundary

For the first organism I should **not** intervene at turns 100, 300, etc. That would make interpretation of the result muddy. One model design intervention creates the laws and seed. The deterministic organism runs 1,000 turns. Then I inspect what actually happened.

Only after that evidence should I decide whether a second model intervention is useful.

This is important: I am not secretly scripting 1,000 authored events. I am writing a small causal machine and accepting whatever trajectory it produces.

## Pass 2 — executable plan

The first build should therefore be one self-contained world organism placed on the empty post-collapse substrate, with:

- fixed seed;
- modest lattice;
- four scalar fields;
- deterministic drifting forcing plus rare shocks;
- local transport that writes durable history;
- conservative anonymous distinction detection;
- lineage/death for distinctions;
- deterministic 1,000-turn history;
- projection derived from state;
- accelerated visible genesis;
- a compact inspectable evidence record after completion.

No generalized framework. No terrain system. No agents. No prose generator. No ontology.

### Falsification conditions

I will count the first attempt as weak or failed if:

- the visible result is mostly initial-condition geometry;
- activity equilibrates and later turns cease to matter;
- traces look structured but do not alter future dynamics;
- anonymous handles merely memorize stable locations;
- the projection implies categories the state has not earned;
- replay is visually interesting but causally vacuous;
- I find myself wanting to add decorative systems to make it feel alive.

If it fails, preserve the specimen and diagnose the pressure. Do not decorate it into success.

## Pass 2 prediction

My strongest prediction is that the first world will be visually sparse and somewhat alien, but that its mature state will contain a small number of path-dependent structures whose future behavior differs measurably from a virgin field.

My uncertainty is the handle criterion. It may be too ambitious for the first 1,000 turns and may teach us that **history can precede things**.

That would still be a useful world.
